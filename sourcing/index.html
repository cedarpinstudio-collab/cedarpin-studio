FAQ

Quick answers.

Is the first search free?

Yes, the first basic product opportunity search is used to understand the request and available sourcing options.

Do you buy the goods?

Not in this initial service. CedarPin provides independent sourcing research first.

Can I send a product link?

Yes. A product link, photo reference, model or part number can all help.

Do you guarantee suppliers?

No. Supplier claims, samples, certifications, shipping and payment terms must be verified before purchase.
