FAQ

Clear from the start.

Do you buy the goods for me?

Not through this initial service. We research and organize sourcing options first. Any additional service is agreed separately.

Is the first search really free?

The first basic product search is offered as an initial opportunity check. Larger research, verification or negotiation work is scoped before paid work begins.

Can I request any product?

We can review many ordinary legal commercial products. We may decline products with regulatory, safety or sourcing concerns.

Do you guarantee the supplier?

No. Supplier claims, samples, certifications, compliance, payments and shipping terms should be independently verified before purchase.
